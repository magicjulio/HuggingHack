ASCENT sound kit

Every sound is synthesised in the browser by prototypes/end2-ascent/audio.js, no sample files. Click a row to hear it. The spectrograms and levels come from an offline render of the exact same code, so they double as the test: a sound that is silent, clips or contains NaN is flagged red. Press M in the game (or here) to mute.
