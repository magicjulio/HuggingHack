# Level 5 revision — Moving the Goalposts

Much harder: a four-position relay, a separated return circuit, and an overhead opposed scanner. No in-game instructions or perch markers. This replaces the previous short two-scanner climb with a complete out-and-back puzzle.

## Intended route

1. Leave KAM under the spawn roof. Ghost climbs the x5.2 ladder to top8.5 and holds the patrol eye leftward. KAM crosses the floor to the central shelter at x16.
2. Drop the ghost to the floor and take it to the far staircase: x31.5/top2.4, far roof/top4, x31/top6, then x27/top7.5. Hold the broad eye down and right. KAM crosses to x31/floor under the far roof.
3. Move the ghost off the left side of its perch and roof, landing around x25.5/floor. Its steep downward lock clears the far staircase. KAM climbs x31.5/top2.4, roof/top4, x31/top6, x31/top8, x30/top10. Wait here behind the solid screen at x28; do not cross it yet.
4. Ghost climbs the far stairs to x30/top12, jumps onto x28/top13, then x23/top12, x20/top12, the central block/top12, x12/top14, and finally x16/top16. Wait until the overhead opposed watcher acquires the ghost above itself. Its opposite downward beam terminates on the central block.
5. KAM climbs x30/top12, x28/top13, x23/top12, x20/top12. Drop through the thin x20 ledge to the top8.5 underpass, walk below the opaque central block to x13, then climb x13/top10.5, x9/top12, x5.5/top12, and the wall at x4/top13. Drop to the left exit ledge at x2/top8.5.

KAM must remain behind the right screen while the ghost changes the overhead watcher's angle. Both the broad eye and overhead watcher have their view to x30/top10 intercepted by that screen. The patrol eye cannot reach that staging point.

## Shortcut closures and geometry

- Hanging left wall extends from y5 to13. Starting floor passes beneath it, but neither the spawn roof at4 nor the setup ladder at8.5 can jump onto its top13. The exit is physically isolated on the left side.
- The return step at x13/top10.5 is too far from the setup ladder. There are no x9.5/top8.5 stepping stones to bridge that gap. The highest nearby left ladder is3.5 below the return platforms at12, beyond jump height.
- The central upper block intercepts the final downward twin beam. Walking across its top would still cross that beam, so KAM must use the lower underpass and climb separately on the left.
- The right perch is at7.5, not8.5, giving a genuinely downward broad-eye lock that blocks the staircase until the ghost drops to the floor.
- The two lower scanners' sweep limits leave the upper underpass/return above their cones. This allows the last ghost position to hold the overhead watcher while the others resume sweeping.
- Platforms are normal-width; intended jumps rise at most2, except the initial2.5 jump to the right floor step. Difficulty comes from staging and occlusion rather than shrinking platforms or speeding every watcher.
