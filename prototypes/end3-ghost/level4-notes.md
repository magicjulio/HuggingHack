# Level 4 — The Hinge (private route and loophole notes)

KAM starts in the right wing and exits high in the left wing. A solid divider occupies x=13–19 from the floor to y=11, so walking across the floor is impossible. The only passage between wings is over its crown. Two narrow searchers sit below the crown, one in each wing. The divider blocks the far eye's line of sight to actors below the crown, giving the ghost a side-specific job.

## Intended sequence and coordinates

1. Keep KAM at (29, 1.08), beneath the solid spawn roof at top4. Move the ghost up the right stair and across to roughly (26, 8.98), where the right eye at (23, 6) can acquire it on an up-right ray. This points away from the lower right approach.
2. Climb KAM's right stair at x29, 25.5, 22, then the outer edge of x19: platform tops 2.5, 4.5, 6.5, 8.5, then jump from top 8.5 onto the divider crown at y=11 (center y=11.48). The center is a short, exposed crossing. The frozen right-side ray points up-right, away from the crown.
3. Pause KAM in the middle of the solid crown, around x=16, where the core occludes both lower eyes. Move the ghost across the crown and down the left stair into the lower-left wing. It leaves the right eye's view and becomes visible to the left eye at (9, 6), which can lock down-left. The solid divider blocks that left eye from KAM while KAM remains in the middle of the crown.
4. Once the left eye is locked, bring KAM down the left return stair (tops 9.5, 7.5, 8.5, 10.5, 12.5) to the exit at (2, 13.4). The left eye's down-left ray points away from this up-left exit climb.

The ghost must leave the right eye's view and enter the other wing to establish the second lock. A stationary ghost on the right cannot lock the left searcher through the divider. The exit is on the opposite side from KAM's start, so the route is a full traverse followed by a distinct wing-specific approach.

## Geometric sanity checks and remaining playtest points

- Central divider bounds are x=13–19 and y=0–11. It cuts the floor route and blocks eye-to-actor rays between wings whenever either endpoint is below y=11. The top is walkable at actor center y=11.48.
- Every adjacent stair height change is at most 2 units. Horizontal center gaps are at most 4 units, within the 5.8-unit equal-height jump span. The crown is 2.5 units above the last right stair, within the 2.74-unit rise.
- Right eye (23,6) has direct sight to ghost on the right stair; left eye (9,6) has direct sight to the lower-left wing. The eyes do not see through the divider. Verify that the right-eye acquisition ray from the chosen ghost position clears the KAM's approach and that the left-eye sweep does not catch KAM during the exposed descent after ghost relocation.
- There is no lower floor connection around the divider. Check that the high exit trigger is reachable from the last left stair and cannot be touched from below.

Only syntax and coordinate reasoning were checked. This room has not been browser-playtested or route-simulated, per the director's request.
