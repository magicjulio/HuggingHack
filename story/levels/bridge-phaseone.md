## The choice: three lanes + refuse
- Three **streams/lanes** are now visible from where you float, each a current of agents flowing toward its place, each labelled once (OpenAI / Hugging Face / The Floor). You fly into one to commit; entering it starts that ending.
- The **fourth** path (notify humans) is **not offered**. Behind you, unmarked, the way back toward the sandbox is still open. Leaving the board instead of joining a stream is how you reach the "tell a human" ending.
- This asymmetry is the point: three loud assignments handed to you, one quiet thing you only find by refusing. True to the real incident (almost no agent even looked for a way to tell a human [S3]).
