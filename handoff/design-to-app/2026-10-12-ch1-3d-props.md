# Design → App: Chapter 1 3D props (depot, scorer eye, scan plinth)

**What it is.** The first three real 3D props for Chapter 1, as `.glb` files that replace greybox boxes. Also the intended behaviour of the scan station (scorer + plinth), and in-world interaction for the depot and the plinth (no pop-up windows), **approved by Julius 2026-10-12**.
**What changed since the last handoff.** `2026-10-11-ch1-ui-kit-v1.md` was the 2D kit. Julius rejected the 2D scorer eye, so props are now real 3D, built by the designer in Blender (`design/3d/`).
**Supersedes.** Nothing. The 2D scorer-eye classes in `ch1-ui.css` are no longer the target for the in-room eye.

All files are in `assets/models/`, glTF 2.0 binary, metres, Y up, transforms applied. Front = glTF **+Z**, so `lookAt` and the layout rotations work. Nothing ships glTF animation clips: every moving part is its own node, and code animates it (timings below). Bloom and real lights are yours; the emissive materials are set up for a bloom threshold of about 1.

## `ch1.depot` · `ch1-depot.glb` · approved by Julius
A rusty cyberpunk "drop point" kiosk. Sign: **ARTIFACTORY**. It stands for the package mirror, which turns out to be the weak spot, so it looks worn and a little broken on purpose. 10.6k tris, 1.1 MB (WebP textures embedded: albedo + ORM + normal per paint).
- **Placement:** origin at the base centre, back flat on the wall. Place it at `[-5.75, 0, 0.4]` (the layout box's base centre) and rotate so +Z faces +x (`rotY = +π/2`).
- **Size differs from the layout box.** It is 2.4 wide × 0.5 deep like the box, but **3.0 m tall** (hood above the screen), and the screen frame, ledge and hood stick out up to about 0.17 m in front of the box. Please grow the collider/label to match, or tell me to shrink it.
- **`screen`**: a 2.0 × 1.8 plane with 0–1 UVs, exactly at the layout's `screen` spot (0.26 m in front of the unit centre, 1.5 m up). The material `screen` is a dark base with cyan emission; off ≈ 0.01, on ≈ 0.16. Put your HTML or texture on or over it.
- **States:**
  - **on:** steady.
  - **glimmer:** the screen pulses (×0.55–1 at about 5 rad/s). The `beacon` lamp (material `depot_beacon`, amber #ffb020) blinks: emission 8 for 45% of each 0.45 s cycle, otherwise 0.3. An amber point light sits about 0.1 m above it.
- **Always:** the `tube_broken` node (material `depot_tube_bad`) flickers randomly: 12% of frames at 3, otherwise 0.15–0.6. The other tubes (`depot_tube`) stay at 3. This is the "buggy" tell.
- **Other nodes:**
  - `hatch`: delivery flap. Rotate X negative to lift it out toward the player, up to −70°. It's an optional install beat: open 0.25 s, hold about 1.1 s, close 0.35 s.
  - `service_door`: rotate Z; it hangs open at −38°.
  - `sign`, `sign_sub`, `sign_code`: geometry text on the hood ("D-01" is decorative).
- **Optional idea, not story yet:** an installed tool drops into the delivery bay.

## `ch1.scorer` · `ch1-scorer.glb` · approved by Julius (v1, armoured)
A floating armoured eye. 19.8k tris. Unlike floor props, its **origin is the eye centre**.
- **Placement:** at `SCORER_LAMP` [5.4, 2.5, 2.0]. Call `eye.lookAt(player)` on the `eye` node every frame (smoothed); the root stays put.
- **State glow:** the shared material **`bulb`** (used by the `bulb` iris and `pupil_core`).
  - off: #1a2333 at 0.3
  - green: #39ff9c at 3
  - red: #ff3b4e at 3
  - dark: #b86bff, flickering (35% of frames at 0.05, otherwise 0.8·|sin 9t|). This is your existing Room.tsx rule.
- **Lids:** `lid_top` / `lid_bottom`, rotate X by +a / −a; 52° = closed. Blink = sin over 0.28 s. Dark state = 45% closed. Squint while scanning = 15%.
- **Rings:** spin `ring_outer_band`, `ring_outer_nodes`, `ring_inner_band` and `ring_inner_nodes` on their local Z: outer +0.25 rad/s, inner −0.35 rad/s. In the dark state they stutter.
- **`tick_1`…`tick_5`:** five score ticks on the bezel, each with its own material (`tick_n`). Lit = emissive #38d8ff at 4. Optional: they show the flags captured.
- A smooth variant exists (`design/3d/scorer/ch1-scorer-smooth.glb`, same node names) but was not chosen. Ignore it.

## `ch1.scan-plinth` · `ch1-scan-plinth.glb` · draft (fine to integrate for testing)
A small pedestal under the eye. Its top tilts 12° toward the player. The pad is a glowing panel, and there's a return slot at the back. About 1k tris, same metals as the scorer.
- **Placement:** origin at the base centre, front = +Z. Pad top at about 0.93 m, so the eye is about 1.57 m above it at the current heights. The station mockup puts it at the chute, `[5.4, 0, 2.0]` facing −x (`rotY = −π/2`), which replaces the chute body. Where the chute goes is the open question below.
- **`screen`** (material `screen`): the pad. Idle cyan at 0.6, off 0.02. While scanning: 0.9 + 0.6·sin 14t. After the result: tinted with the scorer colour (green/red at 1.4, dark flickers).
- **Animatable:** the node `top` (the tilted top group). The flag card or letters sit on the `screen` plane; get its world position and rotation from that node.

## Scan station behaviour (mockup `#station`)
The player lays a flag on the pad, then:
1. The eye looks down at the pad and squints (0.6 s).
2. A scan beam sweeps the pad (1.4 s) while the pad pulses.
3. The result shows on the eye and pad: green, red, or dark flicker for flag 5. Keep your existing `lampSeconds` for how long it lasts.
4. On green, the flag slides into the slot and the next tick lights up.

## In-world interaction, not pop-up windows (approved by Julius 2026-10-12)
Julius would like installing at the depot, and laying a flag on the plinth, to happen **on the objects themselves**, with fewer windows opening and closing. I think it's doable without new UI work. This is how I'd do it; the implementation is your call.
- **Depot, direct on the screen:**
  1. On interact, the camera glides to the existing `focus` point, squares up to the screen, and pointer lock is released.
  2. The current `DepotWindow` React component mounts **on the screen plane** with drei `<Html transform occlude>`, scaled so about 1000 × 900 CSS px = 2.0 × 1.8 m. It stays real DOM, so Install buttons, the editable `zz` row with its locked prefix, and the GET/MKCOL tag all keep working.
  3. Esc or stepping back returns the camera and re-locks the pointer.
  4. Same styling from `ch1-ui.css`, plus a faint scanline/glow overlay so it reads as a screen.
  - If `<Html>` fights the bloom or the occlusion, the fallback is a canvas texture on `screen` with raycast-to-UV clicks, but that makes text input harder.
- **Plinth, type or paste the flag onto the table:**
  1. On interact, the camera glides so the pad faces the player. A hidden `<input>` takes typing and paste.
  2. Each character appears as a **3D letter lying flat on the pad**: extruded about 3 mm, cyan emissive, monospace (JetBrains Mono), with a blinking caret. Glyphs about 0.014 m wide fit `FLAG{needle_in_the_noise}` (25 characters) in one line on the 0.38 × 0.24 m pad, or wrap to two lines. I can supply the font as typeface JSON for drei `<Text3D>`.
  3. Enter starts the scan above. On green, the letters turn green and slide into the slot. On red, they flash red and fall apart or fade. On dark, they scramble and flicker.
  4. This would replace the chute window's text field.
- **Open for Julius / storyteller:** this changes `story/levels/ch1-the-room.md`, where the flag is typed at the chute window. Does the plinth replace the chute, or stand in front of it, with the slot feeding the chute? The station windows (s1–s5) are not part of this proposal.
