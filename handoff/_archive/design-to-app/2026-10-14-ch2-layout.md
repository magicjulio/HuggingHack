## Answers to your §2
1. **Outlines:** `models.{s,m,l}` = `{file, radius, outline: [[x,z]…], slab: 0.45, depth}`. The outline is the same polygon as the glTF extras. **Pull the collider in to 96 %** before rotY/pos (my page does this so you don't stand on the rim lip). `depth` (3.2 / 6.0 / 10.0) is the crystal underside, looks only. Collide with the top only.
2. **Curved bridges:** centripetal Catmull-Rom, exactly `new CatmullRomCurve3(points, false, 'centripetal')`, sampled with `getSpacedPoints(ceil(length / 1 m))`. It's the same library, so you get identical points. Polyline bridges keep exact corners.
3. **Motion:**
   - Slabs now carry explicit `period` and `phase`. They move by `drift·sin(2πt/period + phase)` along the slab's local x (its long side).
   - Lifts use `k = .5 − .5cos(2πt/period)`, `y = lerp(bottom, yTop, clamp(1.25k − .125))`, so they dwell at both ends.
   - Clouds move by `pos + drift·sin(2πt/period)`. Your reading was right.
   - Tilting islands: tilting starts on `tilt: true`. **New: `tilt: "round3"`** on the four forge edges, which stay static until round 3. Numbers are in `physics.tilt`.
4. **Reset waves (changed):** Julius wanted "real red lasers … like the laser scanner from the sandbox scorer".
   - Each wave is a red scan line across the bridge, `width + 1` m long, moving **along** the segment.
   - **Timing:** it runs from −`span` to +`span` m around point `t` in `sweep` s (8 m in 1 s), then rests at that end until `period` is over. The next pass runs back.
   - **Hit:** `|along − line| < 0.35`, `|lateral| < width/2 + 0.3`, `−1 < y − lineY < 3`.
   - **Dash** passes through.
   - **Telegraph:** in the last 1.2 s of the rest a dotted red line marks where the next pass starts, and the emitter charges.
   - Your greybox's "down the bridge for 0.9 s every 2.8 s" was close, so swap the numbers for the JSON's.
   - Formula: `notes.waves`. Code: `waveState()` and `waveHit()` in level.html.
5. **Missing data, added:**
   - `tokens: {value 3, hover 0.9, radius 1.1, spots[24]}` (ground points).
   - `forgeFight`: `bitSpawns[8]` (2 m above the island, between the edge platforms), `waveLanes[2]` (the same scan line, across the forge island, E–W and N–S alternating every 1.8 s), `clouds[2]` (round 3), `tiltFromRound: 3`.
   - `forge`: `slotPos[4]` (frame centres, input order = `slots`), `pylon` footprints (radius 3, 0.9 × 0.5 m, so give them colliders) and `dais` tiers (r 3.5 → +0.9, r 4.8 → +0.5).
6. **Physics:** use the block as the game's numbers. Added: `groundAccel 14`, `airAccel 7`, `coyote 0.12`, `dashCooldown 0.55`, `stepUp 0.45`, `snapDown 0.6`, `padRadius 1.3`, `tilt {max .22, sink .35, rate 3, sinkRate 2.5}`. Glide = Space held while falling. Definitions: `notes.physics`.
7. **Crates:** the six labelled spots stay. `crateSpares` has 6 free floor spots on the Yard for anything else the player installed.
