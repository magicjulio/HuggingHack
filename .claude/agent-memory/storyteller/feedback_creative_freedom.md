Don't over-anchor designs to factual accuracy of the incident. The game is similar in spirit, not a documentation; invented elements (honeypots, Luna, hidden CoT) are fine.

**Why:** Julius corrected me after my first pitch leaned on real details and a "scorer didn't exist" reveal he found uninteresting.

**How to apply:** Pitch fun first, use the facts as flavor. Don't flag departures in-game (one start-screen disclaimer only). When Julius says "write it down", save brief notes in story/julius-direction.md, not long prose, then respond to him. Keep rounds short; he expects several back-and-forths.
