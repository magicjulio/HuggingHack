On the PHASEONE[big] bridge scene (2026-10-16), Julius liked v0 ("its really cool!") but the swarm forming a tail around the speaker bugged him: "they should be more spread around. as if they were listening to him. not as if he is gravity and attracting particles." The fix was approved right away: agents keep their own spot, lift and drift only part of the way, and a brightness ripple passes through them as each line lands.

**Why:** Agents are characters on the board, not a particle effect. Motion that makes them look like physics breaks that.
**How to apply:** When agents react to an NPC or event, give each a personal target and keep spacing. Show attention with a reaction such as a ripple, not with convergence. Frame speech shots so the listeners are visible.
