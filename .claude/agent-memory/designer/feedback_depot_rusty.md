Depot v1 (clean dark metal + cyan, same family as the scorer) was "too modern / clean". Artifactory is the part that turns out buggy, so Julius wants a rusty, grimy cyberpunk look: Cyberpunk 2077 "drop point" kiosks and vending machines (chipped yellow/white paint, a red ribbed base, hazard stripes, glowing chevrons, stencil sign). He asked for the sign to read ARTIFACTORY.

**Why:** the look should tell the story: the mirror is the weak, broken part of the system.

**How to apply:** match a prop's wear to its role in the story. Don't default every prop to the clean scorer family. For worn looks, generate paint/rust/grime textures in numpy inside the build script (see design/3d/HOWTO.md). Related: [[feedback-visual-quality]], [[feedback-scorer-approved]].
